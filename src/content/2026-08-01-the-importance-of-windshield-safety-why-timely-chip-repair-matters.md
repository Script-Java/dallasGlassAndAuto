# Introduction to Windshield Safety
Windshield safety is a critical aspect of vehicle maintenance, often overlooked until a chip or crack appears. At Dallas Glass & Auto, we stress the importance of timely chip repair to prevent further damage and ensure driver safety.
## Why Chip Repair is Crucial
* **Prevents Further Damage**: Chips can spread quickly, especially when exposed to extreme temperatures or bumpy roads, leading to costly replacements.
* **Maintains Visibility**: Unrepaired chips can obstruct the driver's view, increasing the risk of accidents.
* **Saves Time and Money**: Repairing chips early on is faster and more affordable than replacing the entire windshield.
## The Process of Chip Repair
Chip repair involves cleaning the damaged area, applying a resin to fill the chip, and sealing it with a UV-resistant material. This process can usually be completed within 30 minutes to an hour.
